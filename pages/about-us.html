About BEing Civil

BEing Civil IIEST is a student-run initiative from the Civil Engineering department at IIEST Shibpur. It began in 2018 as a way for seniors to pass class notes, lab files and previous-year papers down to juniors, and has since grown into a standing archive maintained by volunteers each year.

Everything on this site is contributed voluntarily. Ownership of each document remains with its original author, and the archive exists purely to help students study — never for commercial use.
